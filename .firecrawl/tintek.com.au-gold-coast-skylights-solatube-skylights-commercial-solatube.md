**The SkyVault Series is a groundbreaking line of tubular daylighting devices designed specifically for large volume commercial spaces with high ceilings like airports, convention centres, lobbies,**

The core of the new SkyVault Series is the Solatube M74 DS Daylighting System, which captures natural light at the roof level, and transfers it indoors using the world’s most reflective material. The base unit provides a 74 cm opening to the sky, allowing more light to enter an area for higher daytime illuminance and / or greater spacing between units with fewer roof penetrations.

The SkyVault Series gives building designers a Solatube daylighting solution that delivers maximum output while minimising impact on the building envelope and significantly reducing installation costs.

## SkyVault Series Benefits

• Maximises light collection for greater daytime brightness

• Provides exceptional thermal efficiency year-round

• Enables wider spacing with fewer roof penetrations

• Reduces labour and waste through smarter installation design
