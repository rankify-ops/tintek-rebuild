# VELUX VCM

The VELUX VCM Opening Skylight is a cost-effective way to bring both natural light and fresh air into rooms with flat roofs. Designed for easy manual operation, it helps improve ventilation while maintaining energy efficiency.

Every unit comes with a pre-installed insect screen, so you can enjoy cool breezes without worrying about mosquitoes or flies during summer.

**Product Features**

‎

• Low-maintenance white PVC interior frame provides a tough, durable finish.

• High-performance double glazing with NEAT™ outer coating helps reduce cleaning frequency.

• Supplied with a removable insect screen for added comfort.

• Grey outer aluminium cappings for protection and durability.

• Available in 7 different sizes.

• Operated with a winder handle for skylights within reach, or by extension rod for higher installations (sold separately).

• Requires custom-made flashing (not supplied by VELUX).

• Blocks approximately 75% of external heat.

## Solar-Powered Blockout Blinds Available

• Fully self-contained design with no external components.

• Built-in battery pack enables operation both day and night.

• Cuts heat transfer by around 40%.

• Simple to install with the VELUX fitting system.

• Operated via a wireless wall-mounted keypad (included).

## High-Performance Double Glazing

• Outer layer: 3mm toughened Low-E3 coated Cardinal glass.

• Cavity: 9mm sealed Argon gas space for insulation.

• Inner layer: 5.36mm clear laminated Cardinal glass with 0.76 PVB interlayer.

• NEAT™ coating on the outer pane helps reduce cleaning frequency.

• Warm edge technology improves overall energy efficiency.
