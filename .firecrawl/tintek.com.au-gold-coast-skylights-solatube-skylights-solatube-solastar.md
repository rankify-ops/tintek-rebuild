# Solatube Solar Star – Solar Roof Ventilation

Solar Star is the Solatube-brand ventilation system. Solatube have taken their expertise on solar power and their wide range of flashings for different roof types and developed Solar Star.

Solar Star is a solar panel-powered rooftop fan for roof cavity ventilation, helping your home maintain a comfortable and consistent climate.

‎

Solatube Solar Roof Ventilation

‎

Solar Star is entirely powered by solar voltaic panels, requiring no electricity. That means that you won’t have to hire an electrician or go to the trouble of bringing electrical wiring up to your roof cavity. The solar panels power an efficient motor that spins the lightweight, low resistance, rust-proof polymeric fan blades in the roof-mounted roof space fans. Hot air exits the roof cavity through a durable and rust-proof polymeric exhaust screen that is fully integrated with the unit.

‎

Solar Star will work in both sun and shade, but if you plan to install one in a location where direct sunlight is rare, we can provide a booster solar panel to increase its effectiveness. The brighter the sunlight, the faster the fan. In the summer, that means the roof cavity gets the most ventilation when it’s absorbing the most solar radiation — helping to keep your home cooler in the summer months, and maintain a comfortable climate.

## Roof Ventilation

Roof ventilation is essential for driving heat and moisture out of the roof cavity throughout the year. It helps keep your home cooler in summer and protects your roof space during the colder months.

‎

Traditional wind vents typically move only 85–100m³ of air per hour (about the extraction rate of a 250mm unit), which is relatively inefficient. This is similar to removing a tile from your roof and covering it with a static cap. While 1–4 wind vents will have some effect, effective ventilation requires about 3 air changes per hour (5 is ideal). For most homes, this means 6–8 wind vents. By comparison, a single Solar Star unit exceeds this level of ventilation — even on a cloudy day. In fact, the cost of installing three quality wind vents is about the same as one Solar Star.

# Roof Mounted Roof Space Fans

Installation and Operation

Solar Star is installed into your roof using the same type of flashings as our tubular skylights. These flashings are designed for almost every flat and pitched roof in Australia, ensuring a leak-proof and simple installation.

‎

• Suitable for flat and pitched roofs across Australia

• Leak-proof flashings for all roof types

• Installed in around 30 minutes

• Main unit mounted with high-quality stainless steel screws

‎

Appearance and Acoustics

Solar Star is low profile and discreet, so it won’t negatively impact the look of your roof. It’s far more subtle and attractive than a whirlybird, and depending on the model, one Solar Star provides the same ventilation as at least ten whirlybirds. It also operates quietly. You won’t hear it from inside or outside the home, and since it only runs during the day, there’s no noise at night.

‎

• Low-profile, discreet design that blends into the roof

• Provides equivalent ventilation to up to ten whirlybirds

• Operates silently

• Runs only during daylight hours

‎

An Ideal Solution

Solar Star is designed for Australian conditions, running fastest under direct sunlight when cooling is most needed. At night, when temperatures drop, it provides passive ventilation. It works year-round, in both summer and winter, to help regulate your home’s temperature.

‎

• Runs fastest in bright sun for maximum cooling

• Provides passive ventilation at night

• Year-round performance in both summer and winter

• Helps regulate home temperature efficiently
