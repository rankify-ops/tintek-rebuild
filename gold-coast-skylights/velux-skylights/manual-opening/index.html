Product Overview

Daylight + ventilation, hand-cranked

Velux Manual Opening Skylights combine all the daylight benefits of a fixed skylight with the ability to open and vent. Perfect for kitchens that need to clear cooking steam, bathrooms that need to dump humidity, and any room that benefits from natural airflow.

Operated by a control rod or hand crank — no electricity, no batteries, no maintenance. Pre-flashed for a leak-free install.
